# Rogue Forever

## Welcome to Targar

Thank you for buying Targar! I hope you have as much fun with Targar as I did.

I built this site while leveling Targar in WoW Forever. Now it is yours, to use as a map for the road ahead. Inside you will find:

* **Gear and stats:** best in slot gear for every level milestone, with the reason behind each pick.
* **Abilities:** every rogue ability with tips, and short clips for most of them.
* **Talents and macros:** builds for each spec, and macros you can copy straight into the game.
* **Class matchups:** what each class has, and how to beat it.
* **World map and dungeons:** quests, NPCs and routes on the real game map, plus dungeon maps with bosses and loot.
* **Leatherworking:** crafting Targar's gear step by step. Type Targar's current skill into My skill at the top of that page, and the guide picks up from there.

Click the level pill at the top right to set your level, spec and faction, and the whole site adjusts to you. Most of the data is read from the WoW Forever game client, and anything that might be out of date is marked.

Enjoy the game, and good luck out there!

## Open the site

Open the GitHub Pages link for this repository. To open it on your own PC instead, download the repository and open `index.html`.
